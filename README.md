# Procedural Tourism: A Cooperative Travel Game

*Your adventure, randomly generated.*

Procedural Tourism is a low-key, cooperative travel game designed to encourage exploration, curiosity, and learning. It turns a day abroad—or closer to home—into an improvised adventure through randomly drawn quests, shared conditions, and personal curses.

The destination is the game board. The adventure is the reward.

## How it works

Each round lasts 12 hours, beginning at a time agreed upon by the travelers. Each traveler draws a **Quest**, the group draws one shared **Condition**, and each traveler draws a **Curse**.

| Deck | Cards | Purpose |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Quest | 60 | Discover, explore, and learn something new. |
| Condition | 40 | Change how the group approaches its adventure. |
| Curse | 50 | Introduce a personal complication to work around. |

Travelers cooperate to complete their quests while observing the applicable conditions and curses. Quests are designed for 90–240 minutes of meaningful effort, leaving plenty of time for ordinary vacation activities. There is no competitive scoring or penalty for failure.

## Getting started

1. Read the [Player Rulebook](docs/player-rulebook.md) ([printable PDF](sets/CORE/assets/rulebook.pdf)).
2. Print the three decks or use the Markdown versions to draw cards digitally.
3. Shuffle each deck separately, agree on a round start time, and begin exploring.

## Printable decks

The core edition includes 150 cards formatted for Avery 95272 (2.5 × 3.5-inch poker-size cards, six per US Letter sheet).

| Deck | Printable PDF | Sheets |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Quest | [Quest cards](sets/CORE/assets/cards/quest-avery-95272.pdf) | 10 |
| Condition | [Condition cards](sets/CORE/assets/cards/condition-avery-95272.pdf) | 7 |
| Curse | [Curse cards](sets/CORE/assets/cards/curse-avery-95272.pdf) | 9 |

Print at actual size (100%), in portrait orientation, with long-edge duplex printing if supported by your printer and stock. Disable fit-to-page scaling. Print the alignment test from `sets/CORE/assets/templates/avery-95272/` on ordinary paper and compare it with the card stock before printing the decks.

## Project status

The core edition is content-complete as a first playable prototype. Combination testing and real-world travel playtesting are pending. Future development may include balance revisions, thematic expansions, destination-specific editions, and professional-quality printed cards.

The project is currently intended for private development and playtesting. Public distribution and licensing have not yet been decided.
