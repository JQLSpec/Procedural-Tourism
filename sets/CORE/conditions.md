# Procedural Tourism: A Cooperative Travel Game — Conditions Deck Design and Cards

**Version 2.1 · Complete 40-card core deck · Ready for playtesting**

*Branding revision: official title adopted; gameplay and card identifiers unchanged.*

This reference contains the design framework and playable card text. The Game Design Document v1.2 and Player Rulebook v1.1 govern general play. This edition supersedes the candidate cards and provisional failure-triggered curse rule in v1.1.

## 1. Round structure

A round lasts **12 consecutive hours**. Agree on the start time, draw one Quest per traveler, draw one shared Condition, then draw one Curse per traveler. Read all cards and check compatibility before beginning. Fresh Start, if drawn, is resolved before curse draws. Base quests require 90–240 minutes of meaningful effort; travelers may combine or pursue them separately as allowed by their cards. Conditions are not additional quests. No failure penalty or card effect carries forward into another round.

## 2. Design philosophy

Conditions change **how travelers experience their destination**, rather than what they are trying to accomplish. They encourage improvisation, cooperation, unfamiliar approaches and discovery without turning the vacation into a competition or a chore.

| Deck | Purpose | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Quest | What you must accomplish | Individual |
| Condition | How you experience the adventure | Collective |
| Curse | Additional complications you must overcome | Individual |

Conditions should not necessarily be punitive. Some restrict behavior, some prescribe a different approach, and some grant flexibility or relief.

Mechanical type is independent of category. The following distribution is implemented across the same 40 cards, not an additional deck.

| Type | Description | Cards | Share |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| Restrictive | Prohibits a behavior or resource | 20 | 50% |
| Prescriptive | Requires a behavior or approach | 14 | 35% |
| Beneficial | Grants a privilege, flexibility or advantage | 6 | 15% |

## 5. Condition scope

Every condition card must explicitly state its scope.

| Scope | Meaning | Suitable examples |
|---|---|---|
| Full Round | Applies throughout the agreed 12-hour round, subject to universal exemptions | No Global Chains; discretionary spending; everyday language |
| Quest Activities | Applies only while actively planning or pursuing quests, including associated local travel where relevant | Analog Adventure; Unreviewed; collaboration and transportation restrictions |

Full Round does not mean the entire calendar day. No condition extends beyond the round. Card-specific timing requirements should use round-relative times where practical.

## 6. Universal condition rules

1. **Compatibility:** Conditions should be compatible with the overwhelming majority of quest cards.
2. **Proportionality:** Conditions should not routinely push quests beyond the four-hour target.
3. **Independence:** Conditions should not become additional quests requiring significant dedicated time.
4. **Accessibility:** Safety, health, mobility, dietary requirements, consent and essential communication always take precedence.
5. **Existing commitments:** Flights, hotel transfers, reservations, work obligations and other pre-existing commitments are exempt.
6. **Objectivity:** Compliance should be reasonably easy to determine.
7. **Enjoyment:** Conditions should create interesting decisions rather than tedious administrative requirements.
8. **Respect:** Do not violate local laws, customs or privacy, or pressure strangers into participating.
9. **Conflict resolution:** If a condition makes any traveler's quest objectively impossible, redraw the condition collectively rather than penalizing an individual. Mere inconvenience is not grounds for a redraw.
10. **Expiration:** The condition ends when the 12-hour round ends. No restrictions carry into subsequent rounds unless explicitly established by a separate game mechanic.


### Interaction and adjudication

- Apply universal exemptions first. If a base quest is infeasible, exchange it; if the condition prevents a feasible base quest, exchange the shared condition. If a curse duplicates the condition or creates an unreasonable conflict, exchange the curse. Resolve jointly incompatible curses by agreement or random choice, then recheck all cards.
- Explicit beneficial permissions override only the restrictions named in their rules. They do not waive safety, personal participation, minimum effort or unrelated card requirements. A permission need not be exercised. All travelers still draw curses.
- Quest Activities includes planning after the draw, associated local travel and pursuit; a card may explicitly permit a preparation interval. Full Round applies until the agreed end time, even if quests finish early. Specific one-time effects resolve at the time stated.
- Shared group requirements need be performed only once collectively unless the card says each traveler must act. Individual restrictions apply to all travelers even when separated. No card requires anyone to manufacture purchases, meals or interactions merely to demonstrate compliance.
- Correct accidental breaches where practical. Material uncorrected breaches affect the responsible traveler's quest outcome; genuinely collective requirements apply to all. Never penalize use of an exemption. If no reasonable combination remains, pause or end the round.
- Set exchanged cards aside with discards; reshuffle discards only when the draw pile is exhausted, excluding active cards. Keep valid progress when exchanging mid-round.

## 7. Card format and full deck

Each card has a stable ID, title, category (section heading), mechanical type, scope, rule and clarification. Universal exemptions apply even if not repeated. IDs use `CORE-C-<category>-<number>`; these may be printed on cards. This is a playtest deck, not a claim of field-validated balance.

### Transportation (6 cards)

#### CORE-C-TR-01 — All Aboard

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Use only publicly available shared transit for motorized quest travel. Walking and other nonmotorized travel remain allowed.

**Clarification:** If suitable transit is unavailable, exchange this condition.

#### CORE-C-TR-02 — Slow Travel

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Do not use express or limited-stop services when an ordinary stopping service provides a practical alternative.

**Clarification:** Direct services with no local alternative are allowed.

#### CORE-C-TR-03 — Multimodal

**Type:** Prescriptive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** As a group, incorporate three distinct modes of transportation into useful quest travel. Walking counts as one mode; bus, rail, ferry and bicycle are examples of others.

**Clarification:** Do not take unnecessary rides merely to reach three modes; exchange if three practical modes are unavailable.

#### CORE-C-TR-04 — No Backtracking

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Avoid retracing a substantial travel segment in the opposite direction during quest activities. Choose a different practical route for return journeys.

**Clarification:** Short access paths, dead ends and routes without reasonable alternatives are exempt.

#### CORE-C-TR-05 — The Scenic Alternative

**Type:** Prescriptive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** For the first substantial quest journey, choose a route with a plausible scenic, architectural or cultural point of interest instead of choosing solely for speed.

**Clarification:** The alternative must fit the quest effort target and agreed budget.

#### CORE-C-TR-06 — Freedom of the Road

**Type:** Beneficial  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Each traveler may ignore one transport-mode or route restriction imposed by their own curse for one continuous journey between two quest destinations.

**Clarification:** All other curse effects and quest requirements remain active; if no such restriction exists, this is a free round with no additional condition.

### Navigation & Geography (6 cards)

#### CORE-C-NG-01 — Analog Adventure

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Navigate using physical maps, signs and directions from willing people. Do not use digital maps, GPS guidance or turn-by-turn directions.

**Clarification:** Digital factual research and translation are allowed; essential navigation is exempt.

#### CORE-C-NG-02 — Uncharted Territory

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Choose principal quest destinations in neighborhoods or settlements that no traveler has previously explored on foot or otherwise visited meaningfully.

**Clarification:** Passing through on transit does not count as prior exploration.

#### CORE-C-NG-03 — Go With the Flow

**Type:** Prescriptive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Use a river, coastline, canal, ridge or other recognizable geographic feature to guide at least one useful segment of the shared quest itinerary.

**Clarification:** An accessible parallel street or path counts; never enter unsafe terrain.

#### CORE-C-NG-04 — The Small World

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Agree on a center point before departing. All principal quest destinations must fall within two kilometers of that point, measured in a straight line.

**Clarification:** Travel to and from the area is unrestricted; exchange if a quest requires a wider area.

#### CORE-C-NG-05 — Across the Boundary

**Type:** Prescriptive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Include a useful quest stop across a recognizable neighborhood, district or geographic boundary from your starting area.

**Clarification:** Crossing a bridge, major street or district boundary is sufficient; no international border crossing required.

#### CORE-C-NG-06 — Flexible Footprint

**Type:** Beneficial  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Each traveler may substitute one quest-required geographic setting, destination type or spatial requirement with a locally appropriate equivalent that preserves the learning objective and comparable effort.

**Clarification:** Agree on the substitution before attempting it; no reduction of minimum personal effort.

### Economics & Consumption (4 cards)

#### CORE-C-EC-01 — Keep It Local

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Full Round

**Rule:** Make discretionary retail and dining purchases only from independently operated local businesses or individual vendors.

**Clarification:** Transit, essential supplies and previously booked services are exempt; if ownership is unclear, use reasonable judgment.

#### CORE-C-EC-02 — Frugal Adventurers

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Before quest planning, agree on a modest per-person quest spending cap in local currency. Stay within it, including new quest-related fares, admissions and purchases.

**Clarification:** Exclude prepaid expenses and essential needs. Do not agree to a cap that makes a drawn quest infeasible.

#### CORE-C-EC-03 — The Best Things in Life

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Select only free-to-enter attractions and public spaces as quest destinations.

**Clarification:** Normal transport, food and optional purchases are allowed; mandatory paid entry required by a quest triggers an exchange.

#### CORE-C-EC-04 — Cash in Hand

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Full Round

**Rule:** Use local cash for discretionary in-person purchases whenever the merchant accepts it.

**Clarification:** Do not obtain cash solely at unreasonable expense or risk; cashless merchants and essential transactions are exempt.

### Technology & Information (5 cards)

#### CORE-C-TI-01 — Offline Mode

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Do not conduct live internet research during quest activities. Use saved information, offline maps, signs and printed material. Allow a single preparation period of up to 15 minutes immediately after the draw to save needed resources.

**Clarification:** Essential communications, current safety information, ticketing and necessary translation remain allowed.

#### CORE-C-TI-02 — Unreviewed

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Do not consult online reviews, ratings, rankings or algorithmic recommendations when choosing quest destinations.

**Clarification:** Factual opening hours, maps, accessibility and safety information are allowed.

#### CORE-C-TI-03 — Human Search Engine

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Find destination recommendations through signs, printed guides, visitor centers or willing people rather than search engines, AI or recommendation apps.

**Clarification:** Digital maps and factual lookup of a destination already identified are allowed; no stranger is required to participate.

#### CORE-C-TI-04 — One Device

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** During shared quest activities, only one traveler at a time may actively operate a phone, tablet or computer for gameplay. Pass responsibility freely.

**Clarification:** Passive recording, accessibility aids, essential communications and dedicated cameras are exempt; separate travelers may each use a device.

#### CORE-C-TI-05 — Library Pass

**Type:** Beneficial  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Each traveler may take one research break of up to 15 minutes during which their own curse restrictions on digital research or translation are suspended.

**Clarification:** The break does not remove evidence, navigation or other curse requirements; only otherwise permitted tools may be used.

### Language & Social Interaction (4 cards)

#### CORE-C-LS-01 — When in Rome

**Type:** Prescriptive  
**Scope:** Full Round

**Rule:** When initiating ordinary public interactions, use an appropriate local greeting or polite opening whenever practical.

**Clarification:** Switch languages immediately if requested or necessary; no interaction must be manufactured.

#### CORE-C-LS-02 — Lost for Words

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Before using a translation app for a routine low-stakes conversation, try a short local phrase, gesture or other respectful independent means of communication.

**Clarification:** Use translation immediately whenever accuracy, consent or safety matters; never prolong an awkward interaction.

#### CORE-C-LS-03 — Trust the Locals

**Type:** Prescriptive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Incorporate one relevant local recommendation into the group itinerary. Obtain it from a willing resident, visitor center or locally authored guide.

**Clarification:** The recommendation must fit existing quest plans, budget and boundaries; no mandatory stranger interaction.

#### CORE-C-LS-04 — Cultural Courtesy

**Type:** Prescriptive  
**Scope:** Full Round

**Rule:** Learn three locally relevant etiquette conventions and observe them whenever the corresponding situation arises during the round.

**Clarification:** Do not create artificial interactions to demonstrate etiquette; use reliable local guidance.

### Food & Dining (3 cards)

#### CORE-C-FD-01 — Nothing Familiar

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Full Round

**Rule:** At discretionary meals, each traveler chooses a main dish or substantial food item they have not previously tried.

**Clarification:** Dietary needs, availability, hydration and necessary nutrition are exempt; no one must eat unwanted food.

#### CORE-C-FD-02 — No Global Chains

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Full Round

**Rule:** Avoid international restaurant and coffee chains for discretionary food and drink purchases.

**Clarification:** Local chains, independent venues and necessary food or drink remain allowed.

#### CORE-C-FD-03 — Regional Specialties

**Type:** Prescriptive  
**Scope:** Full Round

**Rule:** Whenever a traveler chooses a main meal during the round, include at least one item associated with the host region or country.

**Clarification:** No extra meals or purchases required; culturally relevant dietary alternatives count.

### Time & Pace (4 cards)

#### CORE-C-TP-01 — Early Birds

**Type:** Prescriptive  
**Scope:** Full Round

**Rule:** Each traveler must begin meaningful quest activity within the first hour of the round.

**Clarification:** Starting with substantive planning or research counts; if existing commitments prevent this, exchange the card.

#### CORE-C-TP-02 — After Hours

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Full Round

**Rule:** Do not begin substantive quest pursuit until the second half of the round. Brief initial planning is allowed.

**Clarification:** Exchange before starting if opening hours, daylight, transit or remaining time make either quest impractical.

#### CORE-C-TP-03 — The Art of Lingering

**Type:** Prescriptive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** At one principal quest destination per traveler, spend at least 30 minutes engaged in observation, learning or exploration rather than merely passing through. A shared stop may count for both.

**Clarification:** This time counts toward ordinary quest effort; do not linger where inappropriate.

#### CORE-C-TP-04 — Second Wind

**Type:** Beneficial  
**Scope:** Full Round

**Rule:** Each traveler may suspend their own persistent or conditional behavioral curse for one continuous period of up to 60 minutes, announced before it begins.

**Clarification:** The curse resumes afterward; redemption and extra quest completion requirements are unchanged. No carryover or future reward.

### Cooperation & Decision-Making (4 cards)

#### CORE-C-CD-01 — The Navigator and the Captain

**Type:** Prescriptive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Assign one traveler to navigation and another to selecting destinations during shared quest activities. Exchange roles after the first principal destination.

**Clarification:** For larger groups rotate roles; assignments never override personal curses, consent or safety.

#### CORE-C-CD-02 — Two Become One

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Pursue all in-person quest activities together as a group. Individual research or documentation may be done separately while together.

**Clarification:** Personal breaks and essential commitments are exempt; exchange if joint participation is infeasible.

#### CORE-C-CD-03 — Helping Hand

**Type:** Beneficial  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Each traveler may delegate one discrete quest completion requirement to a willing companion, provided both agree before it is attempted.

**Clarification:** The quest holder must still participate personally for at least 90 minutes, understand the result and fulfill the core learning objective; no delegation that violates a curse.

#### CORE-C-CD-04 — Trading Places

**Type:** Prescriptive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Each traveler proposes a practical plan for another traveler’s quest. The quest holder uses that plan as a starting point and may adapt it as circumstances change.

**Clarification:** For larger groups assign plans in a circle; the holder retains final consent and responsibility.

### Chance & Serendipity (4 cards)

#### CORE-C-CS-01 — Fate Decides

**Type:** Prescriptive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** For the first meaningful destination decision with at least two equally practical options, agree on the options and select randomly.

**Clarification:** If no such decision arises naturally, exchange the condition rather than inventing an unnecessary trip.

#### CORE-C-CS-02 — First Impressions

**Type:** Restrictive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** Select discretionary quest-related businesses by firsthand impressions at their location, not prior reviews or recommendations.

**Clarification:** Check factual suitability, price, dietary needs and access beforehand if necessary; do not enter unsuitable businesses.

#### CORE-C-CS-03 — The Detour

**Type:** Prescriptive  
**Scope:** Quest Activities

**Rule:** As a group, incorporate one spontaneous stop discovered during quest activities that was not in the initial plan. Spend enough time to learn or notice something meaningful.

**Clarification:** Choose a nearby stop that can contribute to quest effort; no separate lengthy excursion required.

#### CORE-C-CS-04 — Fresh Start

**Type:** Beneficial  
**Scope:** Full Round

**Rule:** Immediately after the condition is drawn, each traveler may discard and redraw their Quest once, independently. Accept the replacement unless ordinary incompatibility rules apply.

**Clarification:** Make the decision before drawing curses. The ordinary exchange rules remain available.

## 8. Playtesting and expansion notes

The six beneficial cards are included in the category totals, not additional cards. Earlier candidates such as Small Business Saturday, Clean Slate and Split Expedition remain possible expansion ideas; they are not active core cards. Second Wind has been redesigned as a temporary curse suspension rather than a reward for an extra quest. Flexible Footprint is a focused implementation of Creative License. No persistent rewards are introduced.

Test urban and rural settings, short and long transit networks, varied mobility and budgets, and combinations with every quest and curse category. Pay particular attention to After Hours with venue closing times, The Small World with Journey quests, One Device with dependency curses, and Analog Adventure with map-dependent quests. Test whether the benefit cards have useful effects often enough, and whether the restrictive cards change decisions rather than simply causing redraws. Record added effort, redraw frequency, expense, compliance disputes, cooperation and enjoyment. Revise cards that routinely push base quests beyond four hours or function as separate errands.

For destination packs, retain the same schema and safeguards, localize transport, etiquette and geography with practical alternatives, and assign distinct set-prefixed IDs. Verify destination-specific facts before publication. Preserve the core category and mechanical ratios for general-purpose editions; document intentional departures in themed packs.

## Guiding principle

**Quests give you purpose. Conditions shape your environment. Curses challenge your ingenuity. The adventure is the reward.**
