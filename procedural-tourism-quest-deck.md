Legacy IDs are aliases for historical records only. Use canonical IDs for new records and expansions. Version 1.1 changes identifiers and aligns round terminology with the current rules; quest objectives and completion requirements are unchanged.

## Universal quest rules

- Each traveler draws one personal quest at the beginning of a 12-hour round, followed by one shared Condition and one personal Curse per traveler. Travelers may work together and combine compatible quests into one outing.
- Every quest requires at least 90 minutes of meaningful effort and is designed to be achievable within four hours, including necessary local travel, research and documentation. Time estimates are planning guides, not guarantees. Waiting idly or padding time does not count.
- If the listed objectives are completed in under 90 minutes, deepen the investigation, explore an additional relevant site, or document further discoveries until the minimum is met.
- Only activities undertaken after drawing the card count. A companion may assist, but each quest holder must actively participate and explain their own discoveries.
- Use maps, translation, research and other tools unless a condition or curse restricts them. Verify important discoveries using signage, reliable references or willing local sources; distinguish observation from inference.
- Keep expenses modest and agree on a round spending limit before drawing. No purchases are mandatory except where a suitable ordinary meal or fare is part of a quest; dietary and budget-compatible substitutions are allowed.
- Respect private property, photography restrictions, cultural etiquette and people's privacy. Never require strangers to participate. Safety, accessibility, health, weather and local law override all game mechanics.
- Exchange a genuinely infeasible quest without penalty if closures, transport limitations, accessibility, conditions or other circumstances make it impractical within four hours. Choose accessible nearby sites; no strenuous activity is required.
- Completion evidence can be photographs where permitted, sketches, tickets, maps, notes or an oral account. No social media posting is required.

## Exploration — 15 cards

### CORE-Q-EX-01 · Beyond the Tourist Map
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Explore a predominantly residential neighborhood absent from your itinerary.  
**Completion:** Visit a public gathering place, an independent business and a point of local interest. Explain three features that make the neighborhood distinctive, including one you would have missed on a conventional sightseeing route.

### CORE-Q-EX-02 · A Roll of the Dice
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Let chance choose an unfamiliar destination.  
**Completion:** Identify six safe, accessible destinations within 30–45 minutes of your location, randomly select one, and explore it for at least 90 minutes. Record three discoveries and investigate the significance of one.

### CORE-Q-EX-03 · The Road Less Traveled
**Estimated effort:** 1.5–3 hours  
**Quest:** Take an indirect route between two public places roughly one kilometer apart.  
**Completion:** Use at least three streets or public paths outside the obvious direct route. Document five discoveries and investigate two. Explain why the detour was worthwhile.

### CORE-Q-EX-04 · The Third Place
**Estimated effort:** 1.5–3 hours  
**Quest:** Discover where residents spend time outside home and work.  
**Completion:** Visit three different gathering spaces, such as a library, park, plaza or café. Spend at least 20 minutes at each and compare how they support community life without intruding on patrons.

### CORE-Q-EX-05 · Follow the Water
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Explore the relationship between a settlement and a local body of water.  
**Completion:** Visit three accessible points along a river, canal, coast, lake or managed waterway. Identify two ways water has shaped local life and investigate one historical or environmental feature.

### CORE-Q-EX-06 · A Tale of Two Neighborhoods
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Compare two contrasting districts.  
**Completion:** Spend at least 40 minutes in each district. Compare architecture, businesses, public space and transportation; investigate one reason the districts developed differently.

### CORE-Q-EX-07 · The Green Thread
**Estimated effort:** 1.5–3 hours  
**Quest:** Discover how nature fits into the built environment.  
**Completion:** Visit three distinct green spaces or public garden areas. Identify three design, planting or habitat features and learn why at least one is locally significant.

### CORE-Q-EX-08 · What's in a Name?
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Investigate the names found on a destination's streets, districts or public places.  
**Completion:** Visit four named locations, discover the origins of at least three names and explain what those names reveal about local history, geography or language.

### CORE-Q-EX-09 · The Market District
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Explore a commercial district that serves everyday local needs.  
**Completion:** Visit four different types of shops or market stalls without needing to buy anything. Identify three locally distinctive goods or services and investigate one connection to regional life.

### CORE-Q-EX-10 · Where the City Changes
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Explore a transition between two types of land use, such as commercial and residential or urban and agricultural.  
**Completion:** Visit three points on or near the transition. Document four observable changes and investigate one factor that explains the boundary.

### CORE-Q-EX-11 · Architecture Safari
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Discover the architectural language of your destination.  
**Completion:** Find five buildings exhibiting at least three architectural features or styles. Identify their likely functions and investigate the origins or practical purpose of three features.

### CORE-Q-EX-12 · The Public Living Room
**Estimated effort:** 1.5–3 hours  
**Quest:** Investigate how public space is designed for people.  
**Completion:** Visit two squares, parks or pedestrian spaces and spend at least 30 minutes at each. Compare seating, accessibility, shelter, circulation and observed uses; identify one design idea worth borrowing.

### CORE-Q-EX-13 · The Unassuming Attraction
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Find a small, overlooked attraction rather than a famous landmark.  
**Completion:** Visit a minor museum, monument, garden or other public attraction and a nearby related site. Learn four substantive facts and explain why the attraction deserves more attention.

### CORE-Q-EX-14 · The Everyday Infrastructure Tour
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Explore the systems that make daily life possible.  
**Completion:** Find public-facing examples of four different systems, such as waste collection, drainage, transit, water or accessibility. Investigate how two work locally and explain one distinctive design choice. Observe only from permitted public areas.

### CORE-Q-EX-15 · One Square Kilometer
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Investigate a compact area rather than chasing distant attractions.  
**Completion:** Choose an unfamiliar area of roughly one square kilometer. Visit five distinct points of interest, covering at least three types of places. Create a small annotated map and identify one connection between your discoveries.

## History — 10 cards

### CORE-Q-HI-01 · Three Ages
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Explore three distinct periods in local history.  
**Completion:** Visit three structures or sites dating to different periods. Establish their approximate dates, original purposes and present roles. Explain a meaningful change across the three periods.

### CORE-Q-HI-02 · The Forgotten Story
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Discover a locally significant historical person or event previously unfamiliar to you.  
**Completion:** Visit two physical locations associated with the story, such as a memorial and museum. Verify four facts and recount the story and its local significance to your companion.

### CORE-Q-HI-03 · Then and Now
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Reconstruct historical views of your destination.  
**Completion:** Find three historical photographs or illustrations, visit their publicly accessible locations and recreate the viewpoints approximately through photographs or sketches. Explain two changes and one continuity.

### CORE-Q-HI-04 · Built on Something
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Investigate an industry or trade that shaped the community.  
**Completion:** Visit two related public sites, such as a former factory, commercial street or museum. Identify what was produced or traded, why it developed here and two effects on the community.

### CORE-Q-HI-05 · In Their Memory
**Estimated effort:** 1.5–3 hours  
**Quest:** Explore how a community commemorates its past.  
**Completion:** Visit three public memorials, commemorative plaques or monuments. Learn whom or what each honors, when it was established and what its interpretation emphasizes. Note one question the memorials leave unanswered.

### CORE-Q-HI-06 · Second Life
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Discover buildings or spaces that have been repurposed.  
**Completion:** Visit three examples of adaptive reuse. Verify their original and current functions, identify one retained architectural feature at each and investigate why one conversion occurred.

### CORE-Q-HI-07 · Before the Modern City
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Find traces of an earlier settlement pattern.  
**Completion:** Locate an old map or historical account and visit three surviving features, such as streets, bridges, walls or public squares. Explain how the earlier settlement influenced the present layout.

### CORE-Q-HI-08 · A Small Museum, A Big Story
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Use a local museum or heritage exhibition to understand one aspect of the region.  
**Completion:** Spend at least 60 minutes examining the collection, select three objects or exhibits that tell a connected story and visit one nearby related location. Explain what the objects reveal together.

### CORE-Q-HI-09 · Lines of Exchange
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Investigate a historical connection between your destination and another region or country.  
**Completion:** Visit two sites associated with trade, migration, cultural exchange or historical travel. Identify what moved between the places and document three lasting influences on local life.

### CORE-Q-HI-10 · The History Beneath Your Feet
**Estimated effort:** 1.5–3 hours  
**Quest:** Read the historical clues embedded in ordinary streets.  
**Completion:** Find five clues, such as date stones, old signage, street alignments or preserved building details. Verify the history of three and arrange them into a short timeline of the area.

## Culinary — 10 cards

### CORE-Q-FD-01 · Anatomy of a Dish
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Investigate a regional dish unfamiliar to you.  
**Completion:** Learn its origins or cultural context, identify three ingredients at a market or grocery store, and taste a suitable version of the dish. Explain how its ingredients or preparation reflect the region.

### CORE-Q-FD-02 · Market to Table
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Assemble a meal or picnic using local market goods.  
**Completion:** Obtain compatible food from three vendors or specialty shops within the agreed meal budget. Learn one fact about each product, assemble the meal and explain why the combination is locally representative or interesting.

### CORE-Q-FD-03 · A Taste of Everyday Life
**Estimated effort:** 1.5–3 hours  
**Quest:** Discover an ordinary local breakfast, lunch or afternoon snack tradition.  
**Completion:** Investigate typical foods, timing and customs using two sources or establishments. Experience at least two components of the tradition and compare it with your usual routine at home.

### CORE-Q-FD-04 · The Tasting Trail
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Sample unfamiliar regional foods along a short route.  
**Completion:** Visit three independent food establishments in different streets or districts. Sample a small suitable item at each, learn one fact about each and describe the differences in flavor, preparation or cultural context. Sharing portions is allowed.

### CORE-Q-FD-05 · One Ingredient, Many Forms
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Follow a locally important ingredient through different preparations.  
**Completion:** Find three distinct products or dishes featuring the same ingredient at two or more locations. Taste at least one suitable example, investigate the preparation of two and explain why the ingredient matters locally.

### CORE-Q-FD-06 · The Neighborhood Bakery
**Estimated effort:** 1.5–3 hours  
**Quest:** Investigate local baking or comparable staple-food traditions.  
**Completion:** Visit two bakeries, confectioners or traditional staple-food shops. Identify four products, learn about two production methods or traditions, and sample one suitable item from each establishment.

### CORE-Q-FD-07 · A Cup of Culture
**Estimated effort:** 1.5–3 hours  
**Quest:** Explore a local nonalcoholic beverage tradition.  
**Completion:** Visit two establishments serving a locally significant drink or two variants of it. Sample suitable options, investigate their preparation and learn two customs surrounding consumption or service.

### CORE-Q-FD-08 · The Seasonal Table
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Discover how the current season influences local food.  
**Completion:** Visit a market and a food establishment or specialty shop. Identify three seasonal ingredients, investigate why they are associated with this time of year and taste one suitable seasonal product or dish.

### CORE-Q-FD-09 · The Regional Rivalry
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Compare two local or regional interpretations of a similar dish.  
**Completion:** Find two establishments offering distinguishable versions, sample suitable portions and investigate the origin of their differences. Compare ingredients, technique and presentation, without needing to declare a winner.

### CORE-Q-FD-10 · Decode the Menu
**Estimated effort:** 1.5–3 hours  
**Quest:** Learn to navigate unfamiliar local food terminology.  
**Completion:** Examine menus from three establishments, identify eight unfamiliar terms and establish their meanings. Order one suitable item informed by your research and explain three useful distinctions to your companion.

## Cultural — 10 cards

### CORE-Q-CU-01 · Lost in Translation
**Estimated effort:** 1.5–3 hours  
**Quest:** Expand your practical vocabulary in the local language or dialect.  
**Completion:** Learn five unfamiliar phrases and use at least three in naturally occurring interactions across two locations, or in self-guided practice with local signage or audio when interactions are unavailable. Learn one relevant etiquette convention.

### CORE-Q-CU-02 · Local Knowledge
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Discover an experience recommended from a local perspective.  
**Completion:** Obtain two recommendations from willing residents, tourism staff or locally authored guides. Follow one recommendation, spend at least 60 minutes exploring it and explain why it differs from your original itinerary.

### CORE-Q-CU-03 · The Artisan
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Investigate a traditional regional craft.  
**Completion:** Visit two relevant public-facing locations, such as a workshop open to visitors, shop or museum. Identify the materials, three production steps and the craft's cultural significance. No demonstration or purchase is required.

### CORE-Q-CU-04 · Same Problem, Different Solution
**Estimated effort:** 1.5–3 hours  
**Quest:** Explore practical differences between everyday life here and at home.  
**Completion:** Find three real-world solutions to ordinary problems at two or more locations. Investigate the reasons for at least two differences and explain what each approach prioritizes without assuming one is superior.

### CORE-Q-CU-05 · Sacred Spaces
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Learn about local religious or spiritual traditions through publicly accessible heritage.  
**Completion:** Visit two appropriate sites or a site and related exhibition. Learn the relevant visitor etiquette, identify three meaningful features and explain one tradition. Observe restrictions and never enter a service or sacred area without permission.

### CORE-Q-CU-06 · The Local Calendar
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Discover how the community marks the passage of the year.  
**Completion:** Identify three locally significant festivals, holidays or seasonal observances. Visit two places connected to them, such as a public venue, shrine or cultural center, and explain the origins and practices of at least two. No live event is required.

### CORE-Q-CU-07 · How Things Are Done
**Estimated effort:** 1.5–3 hours  
**Quest:** Investigate everyday etiquette through ordinary public activities.  
**Completion:** Visit three different settings, such as transit, a shop and a public facility. Identify three conventions, verify their context using reliable local guidance and practice two when appropriate. Avoid treating individual behavior as a national rule.

### CORE-Q-CU-08 · Signs of Identity
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Discover symbols that express local or regional identity.  
**Completion:** Find five distinct public symbols, such as civic emblems, mascots, motifs or signs, across at least three locations. Research the meaning of three and explain how they communicate identity.

### CORE-Q-CU-09 · The Local Bookshelf
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Explore what a community reads and how it tells its stories.  
**Completion:** Visit a bookstore, library or literary exhibition and a second related location. Identify three locally relevant authors, publications or literary works and investigate the subject or significance of two. Translation tools may be used.

### CORE-Q-CU-10 · Play Like a Local
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Investigate a locally popular leisure activity, game or hobby.  
**Completion:** Visit two relevant locations, such as a game shop, public recreation space or cultural venue. Learn the basic rules or practices and local context, then try an accessible version with your companion or observe and explain an example if participation is unavailable.

## Creative — 8 cards

### CORE-Q-CR-01 · Six Frames
**Estimated effort:** 1.5–3 hours  
**Quest:** Tell a coherent story about your destination through photography or sketches.  
**Completion:** Choose a theme, visit at least three locations and create six original images. Sequence and caption them, include two researched contextual facts and present the resulting visual essay to your companion.

### CORE-Q-CR-02 · The Open-Air Gallery
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Discover the artistic character of public space.  
**Completion:** Find five pieces of public art, murals, sculptures or architectural ornamentation across three locations. Investigate the origin or meaning of three and curate a short walking-gallery itinerary.

### CORE-Q-CR-03 · The Travel Correspondent
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Write a short dispatch from an unfamiliar area.  
**Completion:** Visit three distinct locations and gather firsthand observations. Produce approximately 300 words with three photographs or sketches, a practical recommendation and two verified contextual facts.

### CORE-Q-CR-04 · Museum of the Mundane
**Estimated effort:** 1.5–3 hours  
**Quest:** Curate a miniature exhibition of ordinary local objects.  
**Completion:** Find five objects or examples of ephemera across three locations, photographing or sketching them rather than collecting unless permitted. Write a short exhibit label for each, explaining its function and local context. Investigate two in greater depth.

### CORE-Q-CR-05 · The Color of a Place
**Estimated effort:** 1.5–3 hours  
**Quest:** Build a color palette inspired by your destination.  
**Completion:** Explore three locations and document six recurring or distinctive colors in buildings, signage, landscape or crafts. Create a labeled palette and investigate the practical or cultural significance of two examples.

### CORE-Q-CR-06 · Postcard from Somewhere Else
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Create an original postcard for a place that rarely appears on postcards.  
**Completion:** Explore an overlooked area, visit three points of interest and create an original postcard illustration or photograph with a short message. Include three discoveries and verify one local story. Sending the postcard is optional.

### CORE-Q-CR-07 · The Sound of Somewhere
**Estimated effort:** 1.5–3 hours  
**Quest:** Investigate a destination through its soundscape.  
**Completion:** Spend at least 20 minutes listening at each of three contrasting public locations. Document five distinctive non-private sounds through written descriptions or permitted ambient recordings. Investigate the source or significance of two and create a short soundscape narrative.

### CORE-Q-CR-08 · Design Your Own Mini-Tour
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Become the curator of a themed local tour.  
**Completion:** Choose a theme, scout four publicly accessible stops, verify a relevant fact for each and design a coherent 45–60 minute tour. Guide your companion through at least two stops or rehearse the interpretation if traveling separately.

## Journey — 7 cards

### CORE-Q-JO-01 · End of the Line
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Discover what lies at the end of an unfamiliar local transit route.  
**Completion:** Ride to an accessible terminus within roughly 45 minutes, spend at least 90 minutes exploring and document three points of interest. Investigate one connection between the terminus and its surrounding community.

### CORE-Q-JO-02 · The Grand Circuit
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Create a circular journey through unfamiliar territory.  
**Completion:** Plan and travel a loop using at least two forms of transportation, including walking or an accessible equivalent. Explore three unfamiliar stops, learn one fact at each and avoid substantially retracing the principal route where practical.

### CORE-Q-JO-03 · Three Stops
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Explore a destination through one local transit corridor.  
**Completion:** Select a route and explore the surroundings of three different stops for at least 20 minutes each. Identify one distinctive feature at each and investigate why the areas differ or how the route connects them.

### CORE-Q-JO-04 · The Unexpected Excursion
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Take a short trip to a town or district absent from your itinerary.  
**Completion:** Choose a destination within roughly 45 minutes each way. Spend at least 90 minutes exploring, visit a historical point of interest and a local business or public facility, and explain whether it deserves a return visit.

### CORE-Q-JO-05 · Crossing Over
**Estimated effort:** 2–3 hours  
**Quest:** Explore how a physical barrier influences movement and settlement.  
**Completion:** Cross a river, railway, major road or comparable barrier using a legal public crossing. Explore both sides for at least 30 minutes each, compare three features and investigate the crossing's significance to local connectivity.

### CORE-Q-JO-06 · The Scenic Alternative
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Replace a straightforward journey with a more revealing one.  
**Completion:** Choose a destination within easy reach and identify both a direct route and a safe scenic alternative. Travel the alternative, stopping at three points of interest. Investigate two discoveries and explain what the slower route revealed.

### CORE-Q-JO-07 · A Journey Through Time
**Estimated effort:** 2–4 hours  
**Quest:** Follow a surviving historical transportation corridor.  
**Completion:** Identify a former or current trade road, railway, canal, tramway or pilgrimage route. Travel a short accessible section using available public routes, visit three associated points and explain its original purpose and present-day role.

## Playtesting notes

After each round, record the quest ID, actual active duration, approximate expenditure, whether it combined naturally with another quest, and one memorable discovery. Flag cards that consistently take less than 90 minutes, exceed four hours, depend on unavailable infrastructure or feel like chores rather than adventures. Use these observations to refine the deck alongside the completed Conditions and Curse Decks.
