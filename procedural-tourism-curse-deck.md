# Procedural Tourism: A Cooperative Travel Game — Curse Deck Design and Cards

**Version:** 2.1 — Complete 50-card deck  

*Branding revision: official title adopted; gameplay and card identifiers unchanged.*
**Status:** Complete first playable draft; field playtesting pending

## 1. Purpose

Curses are a normal part of cooperative travel gameplay, not a punishment for failure. Each traveler receives a personal complication that encourages ingenuity, adaptability, and cooperation while exploring a destination.

- Quests give travelers purpose.
- Conditions shape the shared adventure.
- Curses challenge individual ingenuity.

A curse should be mildly inconvenient and amusing, never humiliating, unsafe, or so burdensome that it spoils the vacation. Favor asymmetric limitations that allow travelers to compensate for one another over simply adding more work.

## 2. Round mechanics

Each round lasts 12 hours, with a start time agreed upon by the travelers. At the start of every round:

1. Each traveler draws one individual Quest card.
2. The group draws one collective Condition card.
3. Each traveler draws one individual Curse card.
4. Resolve redundant, impossible, or otherwise incompatible combinations before beginning quest activities.
5. Cooperate to complete individual quests while observing the applicable condition and curses.
6. At the end of the round, record quest outcomes and discard or set aside the active cards. All remaining curses expire.

For two travelers, five cards are in play: two Quests, one Condition, and two Curses. A traveler draws a curse every round regardless of the previous round's outcome. Failure does not create an additional curse or any escalating penalty.

## 5. Curse duration and scope

Every curse must state both when it expires and when its restrictions apply. No curse carries over into a subsequent round.

### Duration types

| Type | Expiration |
|---|---|
| Persistent | At the end of the 12-hour round, even if the quest has already been completed. |
| Conditional | When an explicit optional redemption requirement is satisfied, or at the end of the round, whichever occurs first. |
| Quest-bound | Upon completion of the affected quest (or specified one-time effect), or at the end of the round, whichever occurs first. |

A conditional curse offers a choice: endure the restriction or invest a small amount of effort to lift it. For example, **Digital Amnesia — Redemption Variant** prohibits personal search-engine use until the traveler documents three previously unfamiliar local customs. Redemption must remain optional, feasible, and within the additional-effort budget.

### Scope

- **Full round:** Applies throughout the agreed 12-hour gameplay window, except for universal exemptions.
- **Quest activities:** Applies only while the cursed traveler is actively pursuing quest activities, including relevant travel and research.

Duration and scope are independent. A persistent curse with quest-activity scope remains in force until the round ends but does not restrict ordinary vacation activities outside that scope.

## 6. Universal curse rules

1. **Individual application:** A curse applies only to the traveler who draws it. It does not automatically restrict companions.
2. **Mandatory draw:** Every traveler draws one curse every round, regardless of previous success or failure.
3. **Temporary:** All curses expire no later than the end of the 12-hour round.
4. **Meaningful complication:** Each curse must introduce a genuine limitation, obligation, or decision-making challenge.
5. **Proportionality:** A curse should add no more than approximately 30 minutes of additional effort, including any optional redemption activity. This is a design target, not a requirement to spend extra time. Base quests retain their 90–240 minute target; cursed quests should remain manageable within the round and preferably within four hours.
6. **Cooperation:** Assistance from companions is permitted unless explicitly prohibited by a card. Assistance must comply with the collective condition and the assisting traveler's own curse.
7. **Non-transferable:** A traveler cannot give their curse to another traveler. Delegating a permitted task is not transferring a curse.
8. **Non-accumulative:** No traveler may have more than one active curse. There are no stacking penalties or carryover curses.
9. **Respect:** Curses must not require public embarrassment, harassment, disruptive behavior, trespassing, or inappropriate interactions. Respect local laws and customs.
10. **Recoverability:** A cursed quest must remain reasonably achievable without exceptional physical effort, spending, or dependence on unwilling strangers.
11. **Personal autonomy:** Game-related decision authority never extends to personal boundaries, essential expenses, health, or safety. Any traveler may decline an uncomfortable activity.
12. **Universal exemptions:** Safety, accessibility, health, dietary requirements, essential communication, work obligations, and pre-existing travel commitments override curse restrictions. No penalty applies for using an exemption.
13. **No redundant curses:** If a curse merely duplicates the collective condition or adds no meaningful effect, redraw it.
14. **No impossible combinations:** If a curse makes either traveler's quest objectively impossible or creates an unreasonable burden, redraw the incompatible curse rather than abandoning the quest.
15. **Redemption:** Early removal is permitted only if the card specifies an optional redemption mechanism, or a beneficial condition explicitly suspends or removes the curse.
16. **No failure penalty:** An incomplete quest does not trigger additional curses or other penalties in later rounds.

## 7. Resolving card conflicts

Use the following proposed resolution procedure:

1. Apply universal safety and accessibility exemptions first.
2. If the collective condition is incompatible with a base quest, redraw the condition collectively.
3. If an individual curse duplicates the condition, conflicts with a quest, or makes another curse combination impractical, redraw the affected curse.
4. If both curses are individually valid but jointly incompatible, mutually select one to redraw (randomly select if no agreement is reached).
5. Recheck the resulting combination. Mere inconvenience is not a reason to redraw; objective impossibility, unreasonable burden, or safety concerns are.

If a conflict emerges during gameplay because circumstances change, apply the same principles. Travelers should not be penalized for closures, service disruptions, or other circumstances outside their control.

## 8. Example cooperative round

| Card | Traveler One | Traveler Two |
|---|---|---|
| Quest | End of the Line — explore a transit terminus | Anatomy of a Dish — investigate and eat a regional dish |
| Curse | The Accidental Tourist — no personal map use | The Untranslatable — no personal translation-app use |
| Shared Condition | Unreviewed — no online reviews, ratings, or rankings | Same shared condition |

Traveler One can translate signs and menus, while Traveler Two navigates. Together they can explore a new neighborhood, visit a market, and discover a regional dish. Neither is entirely self-sufficient, but the complementary restrictions encourage cooperation.

## 9. Round outcomes

| Outcome | Criteria |
|---|---|
| Successful Expedition | All travelers complete their quests while observing applicable cards. |
| Partial Expedition | At least one, but not all, travelers completes their quest. |
| Misadventure | No traveler completes their quest. |

There is no penalty for failure. An optional expedition journal can record the cards drawn, outcomes, discoveries, photographs, and amusing incidents. A formal scoring or reward system may be developed later if desired.

## 10. Recommended curse card format

Each production card should include:

- Unique identifier and category.
- Title and optional humorous flavor text.
- Clear effect with an observable restriction or requirement.
- Duration type and scope.
- Optional redemption requirement, if applicable.
- Card-specific exceptions or compatibility notes, if needed.

### Example card

> **MN-01 — The Accidental Tourist**  
> *Someone else holds the map.*  
> **Category:** Mobility & Navigation  
> **Effect:** You cannot personally consult maps or navigation applications. Your companion may provide directions.  
> **Duration:** Persistent  
> **Scope:** Quest activities  
> **Redemption:** None  
> **Exceptions:** Essential navigation for safety and existing travel commitments is permitted.

## 12. Outstanding design questions

- Validate the authored 50-card deck in real-world playtesting.
- Determine the representation of persistent, conditional, and quest-bound curses after playtesting.
- Test combinations against the full Quest and Conditions decks, particularly technology restrictions, reciprocal dependencies, and transportation limitations.
- Define discard and reshuffle procedures consistently across all three decks.
- Decide whether expedition outcomes need scoring or whether the journal alone is sufficient.

The guiding principle: a curse should elicit a laugh and a groan, not genuine disappointment.

## 13. Complete playable Curse Deck

All 50 cards below are the authoritative playable versions of the examples in Section 4. IDs use `CORE-U-<category>-<number>` (U distinguishes curses from conditions). Universal exemptions apply to every card. Conditional cards state an optional redemption; all others have none. Quest-bound one-time effects are fulfilled when performed, but the card stays associated with the quest until completion or round end. For any delegated decision in a larger group, designate one willing companion at the start of the round. If an effect has no meaningful opportunity or duplicates another card, exchange it rather than inventing busywork. Track total added effort against the 30-minute target.

### Mobility & Navigation

#### CORE-U-MN-01 — The Accidental Tourist

**Duration:** Persistent  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** You may not personally consult maps or navigation applications. A companion may give directions.

**Clarification / redemption:** Use signage and spoken directions; essential navigation is exempt.

#### CORE-U-MN-02 — The Long Way Around

**Duration:** Quest-bound  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** For one principal destination, choose a safe alternative to the shortest practical route.

**Clarification / redemption:** Limit the detour to 15 extra minutes; exchange if no alternative exists.

#### CORE-U-MN-03 — Forbidden Chariot

**Duration:** Persistent  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** Randomly select one normally useful local transport mode from those with practical alternatives. Do not use it.

**Clarification / redemption:** Do not select an unavailable mode or one required by a quest or condition.

#### CORE-U-MN-04 — The Wandering Soul

**Duration:** Persistent  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** At each principal destination, leave by a different public exit from the one you entered, when practical.

**Clarification / redemption:** Never use restricted exits or make a detour exceeding the effort limit; exchange if no meaningful opportunity exists.

#### CORE-U-MN-05 — The Station Shuffle

**Duration:** Quest-bound  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** Let your companion choose one unfamiliar, practical transit stop or station exit for your approach to a quest destination.

**Clarification / redemption:** The choice must preserve access and add at most 15 minutes; exchange if transit is irrelevant.

#### CORE-U-MN-06 — Compass Confusion

**Duration:** Conditional  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** You may not use turn-by-turn navigation; static maps remain permitted.

**Clarification / redemption:** Redemption: identify two public wayfinding signs and explain where they lead.

#### CORE-U-MN-07 — The Transfer Tax

**Duration:** Quest-bound  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** Make one otherwise useful quest journey using an additional transit transfer rather than a direct service.

**Clarification / redemption:** The alternative must be safe and add no more than 15 minutes; exchange if none exists.

#### CORE-U-MN-08 — Street-Level Explorer

**Duration:** Persistent  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** Do not use underground pedestrian passages or elevated pedestrian shortcuts when an accessible street-level alternative exists.

**Clarification / redemption:** Road crossings must be legal and safe; exchange if no meaningful choice is expected.

#### CORE-U-MN-09 — The Last Mile

**Duration:** Quest-bound  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** For one destination, get off public transit one stop earlier or park at a slightly more distant legal location and explore the approach.

**Clarification / redemption:** Use a comfortable accessible route adding no more than 15 minutes; exchange if unsuitable.

#### CORE-U-MN-10 — The Unfamiliar Approach

**Duration:** Conditional  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** Do not personally select a route you have already traveled on this trip when an equally practical new route exists.

**Clarification / redemption:** Redemption: identify and explain one useful feature of an unfamiliar route.

### Quest Complications

#### CORE-U-QC-01 — Overachiever

**Duration:** Quest-bound  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** Increase one suitable non-time numerical quest requirement by 50%, rounding up.

**Clarification / redemption:** Choose the requirement with your companion; exchange if no increase fits within 30 minutes.

#### CORE-U-QC-02 — The Skeptic

**Duration:** Quest-bound  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** Verify three facts learned during your quest using an independent second source for each.

**Clarification / redemption:** Sources can be signs, exhibits or reliable publications; exchange if verification is impractical.

#### CORE-U-QC-03 — Forbidden Ground

**Duration:** Quest-bound  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** Do not count locations already listed on your original itinerary toward quest completion.

**Clarification / redemption:** You may still visit them for vacation purposes; exchange if no reasonable alternative exists.

#### CORE-U-QC-04 — The Historian's Burden

**Duration:** Quest-bound  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** Learn and record one fact about the historical significance of a location visited during your quest.

**Clarification / redemption:** Choose a fact beyond what the quest already requires; cite the source.

#### CORE-U-QC-05 — The Local Connection

**Duration:** Quest-bound  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** Identify a connection between two quest discoveries and explain it to your companion.

**Clarification / redemption:** Support the connection with observed details or a reliable source; do not invent facts.

#### CORE-U-QC-06 — The Missing Chapter

**Duration:** Quest-bound  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** Identify one unanswered question arising from your quest and investigate it using a reliable source.

**Clarification / redemption:** Record the answer or a documented inconclusive finding after up to 20 minutes of research.

#### CORE-U-QC-07 — The Counterexample

**Duration:** Quest-bound  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** Find one example that challenges an initial assumption about your quest subject.

**Clarification / redemption:** Explain the assumption and what you observed; exchange if no meaningful assumption can be identified.

#### CORE-U-QC-08 — The Extra Exhibit

**Duration:** Quest-bound  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** Make one additional small discovery near a principal quest destination and explain its relevance.

**Clarification / redemption:** It must not duplicate an existing requirement or require a separate lengthy trip.

#### CORE-U-QC-09 — The Comparative Scholar

**Duration:** Quest-bound  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** Compare one quest discovery with a similar example from your home country or a previous trip.

**Clarification / redemption:** Record two similarities or differences grounded in observation or research.

#### CORE-U-QC-10 — The Curator's Burden

**Duration:** Quest-bound  
**Scope:** Quest activities

**Effect:** Select three discoveries from your quest and create a short caption explaining why each deserves attention.

**Clarification / redemption:** Use notes, audio or accessible equivalents; add interpretation beyond existing quest requirements.
